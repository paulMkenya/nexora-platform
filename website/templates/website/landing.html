Questions

Everything you might be wondering.

Is {{ brand.name|default:"Nexora" }} really white-label?
Completely. Each brand runs on its own domain with its own logo, colors and isolated data. Your partners and advertisers never see the platform name — it's your network, end to end.
How do crypto payouts work?
{{ brand.name|default:"Nexora" }} ships a provider-agnostic crypto layer. Run fully self-custodial (you hold the keys, pay network gas directly) or plug in a provider like NOWPayments. Affiliates pick their network — USDT-TRC20, USDC-Polygon, USDC-Base, BTC, ETH — and addresses are validated per chain before any payout.
Do you really support M-Pesa?
Yes — native M-Pesa B2C disbursement via Safaricom Daraja, with result callbacks and reconciliation. Affiliates in Kenya and East Africa get paid in seconds, not days.
How fast can I launch?
Most networks are live the same day. Sign up, configure your brand, add your first offer and start handing out tracking links within an afternoon.
What about fraud?
Fraud scoring is built into the core, not sold as an add-on. Every click and conversion is scored in real time across bot detection, click flooding, proxy/datacenter checks, impossible-geo and velocity rules — with automatic rejection above a threshold you control.
Can I migrate from another platform?
Yes. Bring your offers, advertisers and affiliates. Our team helps map your existing structure so you keep your history and momentum.
